Composition

I write music on commission, both for live performance and as fully produced tracks.

Songs and theatre music

I specialise in composing theatre music, and I'm especially drawn to writing music for songs — over the past few years I've composed more than 70, mostly for stage productions. If you are interested in working together, please get in touch — I will then share a demo tailored to the character of the planned work.

See selected works

Other projects

Outside the theatre, I have also composed popular music, film scores, incidental and programme music, as well as a sound installation. I draw mainly on the sound of acoustic instruments, whether recorded live or sampled.
