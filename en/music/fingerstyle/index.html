Fingerstyle

For over a decade I have been arranging well-known songs for solo guitar in a style that blends fingerstyle with classical guitar.

YouTube

I run a YouTube channel, where I regularly publish recordings of new arrangements. It already features more than 100 pieces, with new ones appearing every few weeks.

Live performances

Beyond my online activity, I enjoy performing live — at civil wedding ceremonies and corporate events, among other occasions. More details can be found in the Live music section, which also includes my full solo repertoire, covering pieces that have not yet been recorded for YouTube.

Repertoire

List of pieces with online materials. If a piece has no link to sheet music, it means I have not written it down. I sell my sheet music through musicnotes.com, though when there are licensing issues I sometimes share it directly as a PDF.
