Live music

What I offer

I've been performing live professionally for several years now. This includes both my solo playing (see the Fingerstyle section) and acoustic duo performances with vocalists. I mainly accompany on guitar, but piano is also an option for shorter sets.

Where I play

A broad repertoire makes it easy to tailor the set to a variety of occasions, such as corporate banquets, civil wedding ceremonies, private events, or regular residencies at hotels and restaurants. I especially recommend combining solo playing with a duo — it strikes a nice balance between gentle background music and a more attention-grabbing highlight in the event programme.

Equipment and formalities

I have professional sound equipment suitable both for indoor spaces and outdoor events for up to around 100 people. I run a registered business and can issue invoices. I'm based in Kraków, but I'm fully mobile.

Vocalists

Dominika Guzek

Musical theatre actress and vocalist, a graduate of the AST National Academy of Theatre Arts in Kraków (vocal-acting faculty). She has appeared in many musicals and music shows in Poland, on stages including Teatr Rozrywki in Chorzów, Teatr Syrena and Teatr Muzyczny Roma in Warsaw. She is currently an actress at Teatr Groteska in Kraków and regularly performs on Kraków's music stages, including Piwnica pod Baranami and Harris Piano Jazz Bar.

More recordings: YouTube playlist.

Sara Gawlik (Salia)

Actress and vocalist; she has collaborated with Teatr Proscenium for over a decade. She performs on Kraków stages in both musical and theatrical projects, including Piwnica pod Baranami, Klub pod Jaszczurami and Teatr Odwrócony. She has honed her vocal craft under many teachers at music workshops and at the Krakow School of Jazz and Popular Music.

Duo's Instagram: @salia_duoband.

Anna Korbicz

Jazz vocalist; currently studying at the Faculty of Music of the University of Rzeszów. Beyond popular music, she draws inspiration from folk traditions and is part of the ensemble Serencza, one of the leading performers of Lemko music.
